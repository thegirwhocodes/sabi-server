## 🎯 What YC Founders Actually Screen For

### In Your GitHub (✅ You have this)
- Evidence of shipping velocity
- Real products with real constraints
- Product thinking, not just code
- Full-stack pragmatism

### In Interviews (Prepare for this)
1. **"What interests you in our company?"**
   - ❌ "I love your mission"
   - ✅ "I tried your product. I noticed [X, Y, Z]. I'd ship [feature] first because [reason]."

2. **Practical debugging**
   - Not LeetCode
   - "Here's a production bug. Walk me through debugging it."

3. **Communication density**
   - "Summarize a recent PR in 3 sentences"
   - They want: what, why, tradeoff
