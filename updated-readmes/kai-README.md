---

## 🎯 The Problem

Traditional Pomodoro timers use a universal 25-minute work / 5-minute break pattern. Research shows:
- Optimal focus duration varies by person
- Performance degrades differently based on fatigue
- Time of day affects concentration capacity
- Rigid intervals ignore individual differences

---

## 💡 The Solution

**Kai** keeps the structure of predetermined blocks and protected breaks, but personalizes the duration based on how you're actually performing.

### Adaptive Timing Engine

Block length adapts based on:
- **Recent focus ratings** — How well did the last few sessions go?
- **Fatigue signals** — Are you tired or fresh?
- **Streak data** — Building momentum or recovering?
- **Time of day** — Morning peak vs afternoon slump
- **Calendar fit** — Available time until next commitment

---

## ✨ Features

### Lock-In Mode
Commit to a total work stretch (e.g., "2 hours"). Kai:
1. Plans the full focus/break sequence upfront
2. Ends on a focus block (no wasted break at the end)
3. Runs the entire sequence hands-free

### Voice Control
Start focus blocks and breaks by:
- Clicking UI buttons
- Keyboard shortcuts
- Speaking naturally ("start focus", "take a break")

### Visual Environments
Curated focus backgrounds:
- Library lamps
- Minimalist desks
- Ambient work scenes
- Calm, distraction-free aesthetics

### Progress Tracking
- Session history
- Focus ratings over time
- Streak tracking
- Performance insights

## 🧠 Research Foundation

Kai's timing engine is built on principles from:
- Attention restoration theory
- Cognitive load research
- Circadian rhythm effects on focus
- Individual differences in concentration capacity

The key insight: **Predetermined structure matters more than universal duration.**
