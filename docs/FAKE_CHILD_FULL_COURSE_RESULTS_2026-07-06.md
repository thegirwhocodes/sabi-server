## Bugs Found And Fixed

1. Short literacy answers were too permissive.
   - `cat` could fuzzy-match `sat`.
   - `a` could match inside `umbrella`.
   - segmented `c a t` could match unsegmented `cat`.
   - Fixed in `answer_matcher.py` with stricter short-text and segmented-answer matching.

2. Clinic vocabulary answer `sick` was being treated like numeric `six`.
   - This poisoned later expected-answer matching for `medicine` and `doctor`.
   - Fixed by separating canonical expected-number parsing from loose child-answer parsing.

3. Literacy wrong-answer pool could accidentally choose the correct answer.
   - Fixed in `fake_child_harness/full_course_runner.py` by filtering wrong answers against expected answers.

4. Numeric grading counted the instructional phrase “Let’s do one together” as a math number.
   - This made `100 - 40` look like `100 - 40 - 1`, so `59` could be graded correct.
   - Fixed in `numeric_grading.py`.

5. Numeracy advancement was too jumpy over long repeated-call simulations.
   - A single 3-correct / 0-wrong call could eventually happen by luck.
   - Fixed in `learning_state.py`: numeracy now needs two mastery-confirming calls for the same lesson before advancing.
   - Confirmation evidence survives a noisy non-mastery call on the same lesson, and clears after the lesson advances.

6. Counting calls were sometimes summarized as addition because “Let’s do one together” contained `together`.
   - Fixed in `infer_skill_from_question`.
